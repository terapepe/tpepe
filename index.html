What $TPEPE does

  • Fund share campaigns

    New memecoin projects open a campaign with a TPEPE deposit and pay the community for every visit and buy click they send.

  • Earn by sharing

    Share your own link for any live campaign and earn TPEPE for every signed-in visitor and buy click.

  • Win the weekly spotlight

    Bid for the featured slot. The winner is promoted on official channels and gets 100 free community shares.

  • Launch with a crowd

    Create a token on terapepe.xyz with community share quests and rewards switched on from the first minute.

How a campaign runs

  1. A project opens a campaign with TPEPE

    It deposits at least 10,000 TPEPE. No holding requirement.

  2. It sets the rewards

    TPEPE per referral visit and per buy-link click, for 1 to 24 hours.

  3. The community shares

    Each signed-in wallet gets its own ref link and share buttons.

  4. Sharers get paid

    Rewards stop when the deposit runs out; unused TPEPE goes back to the project.
